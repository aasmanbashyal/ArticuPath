ArticuPath's first release is a local studio for rehearsing presentations and practicing spoken English.

- Record your voice, camera, or screen, or import a take you already have.
- Practice with 90 cue cards or read your own script from the teleprompter.
- Install an optional offline transcription model. Select a transcript line to jump to that moment, or compare a scripted take to what you planned to say.
- Keep takes on your computer and browse them by date, cue card, or tag.

**Windows 11 x64:** Extract `ArticuPath-windows-x64-standalone.zip` and open `ArticuPath.exe` inside the extracted folder.

**Ubuntu 24.04 x64:** Run `sudo apt install ./articupath_0.1.0-1_amd64.deb`, then open ArticuPath from the app menu.

Also included: the short product demo. Music: “Happy Beats / Business Moves, Vol. 9” by [Sascha Ende](https://ende.app/en), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), trimmed and mixed for the demo.
